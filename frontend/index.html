Convergence of Gradient Descent

Proposition 3.4. Let f be differentiable and strongly convex with parameter m > 0. Assume that its gradient is Lipschitz continuous with constant L.

For any initial point x0 and step size η ∈ (0, 2/L), the sequence generated by gradient descent converges to the unique minimizer.

‖xk − x*‖² ≤ (1 − ηm)k ‖x0 − x*‖²

Proof. By strong convexity and the descent lemma, we have the following estimate for every iteration:

f(xk+1) − f(x*) ≤ ⟨∇f(xk), xk − x*⟩ − m/2 ‖xk − x*‖²

The first-order optimality condition gives ∇f(x*) = 0. Applying the three-point identity to the inner product yields:

2η⟨∇f(xk), xk − x*⟩ = ‖xk − x*‖² − ‖xk+1 − x*‖² + η²‖∇f(xk)‖²

Combining the inequalities and choosing η = 1/L gives the claimed contraction factor.
